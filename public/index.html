Project Recommendation: Scrum + XP Engineering Practices

For the Student Record and Academic Management Platform, the team firmly recommends Scrum hybridized with XP engineering practices. The single cross-functional team topology (5-7 members) benefits from Scrum’s clear roles, 2-week sprint demonstration cadence, and empirical inspection, while XP practices (CI/CD, code reviews, automated testing) guarantee zero regression bugs in critical grade and attendance computations without the heavy bureaucratic overhead of enterprise frameworks like SAFe.
